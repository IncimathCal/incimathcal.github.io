![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p4.webp)

The image above showcases Shanghai Line 15’s southern extension. The Line’s southward extension comprises four stations, from the top to down is Ziguang Road, North Huancheng Road, Oriental Valley Avenue, Wangyuan Road. Between Ziguang Road and North Huancheng Road is Xizha Highway station, an infill station which in its current form is a ventilation shaft for the line’s tunnels. The extension has been officially broke ground on December 24, 2023, and is expected to open for operation at the end of 2028, at the time when the commute between Fengxian New city and the central city will be more convenient. This article aims to explain the planning changes of this line from the initial proposal to the commencement of construction.

Origin

It is impossible to say when the southern extension of Line 15 was initially proposed. But obviously, we can deduce from the line’s ROW that the initial proposal raiser must also happen to be by the biggest beneficiary of this line extension – the borough of Fengxian. Just a river away from Line 15’s phase 1 southern terminus, any forward-thinking public planner working for the borough can certainly see that the line is just at a tube pair’s length beneath the Huangpu River to benefit the borough.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p5.webp)

Soon, it was mentioned in the brough’s 2040 masterplan (initial progress report, 2016.7) that:

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p6.webp)

“At the regional level of external facing public transportation network, while the boroughs of Jiading and Songjiang has already relied on Shanghai-Nanjing and Shanghai-Hangzhou transportation corridors to become the two major regional nodes of external radiation, **the borough’s Nanqiao New city has not yet constructed its own upstanding external facing public transportation network at the municipal level**.

At the city level, Jiading, Songjiang, Nanhui New city (of theBorough of Pudong), is served by Shanghai Metro’s Line 11, Line 9, Line 16 respectively, each providing a direct rapid rail links into the central city. For Qingpu New City (of the Borough of Qingpu), Shanghai Metro’s Line 17 is expected to be opened in 2017. Nanqiao New city only expects Line 5 southern extension line to speed up the connection to the center city. **There is indeed a need of strengthening rapid rail transit links between Nanqiao New city and the center city**.”

Hence, it is presented later in the Comprehensive Transportation Planning and Outlook section of the 2040 Masterplan (shown embedded in the image):

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p7.webp)

“External Facing Transportation Planning: extending planning to include a public transportation system with **conventional railroads (Shanghai-Zhapu Railway), regional subway line (Fengxian-Pudong Line, Caolu-Fengxian Line, Fengnan-Lingang Line) and Metro Line 5 as the main backbones**;extending planning for an general aviation airport to enhance Fengxian's high-end business, emergency response, and other aviation services options; extending planning for the optimization of inland waterway port layouts, enhancement the function of waterways and optimize the passenger and freight traffic composition.”

The Fengxian line (known as the Fengxian-Pudong Line in the image) was proposed as an external connection solution. We can see that the embedded text says nothing about the southern extension of Line 15.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p8.webp)

But when you zoom in and look at the upper left corner of the same map, it's obvious that Line 15’s southern extension was indeed considered – but only lightly – with a one stop extension into Xidu.

At the end of 2016, Shanghai’s Phase III Subway Network planning has published its finding, reflecting the formation of “One Loop, Nineteen Trunks, and Six Peripheral” of its local lines serving the central city and its immediate environs. At the same time, Phase III also reserved 297 kilometers of municipal controlled ROW areas for future local lines, among which, of the southern extension of Line 15 is one of these controlled ROW areas.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p9.webp)

“Taking into account the future development needs of the center city, this phase aims to reserve and control 4 distinct ROW areas for 4 future subway lines; taking into account the future development needs of the new cities, this phase to reserve and control several distinct ROW areas a number of lines in Jiading, Songjiang and other 5 new cities; **this phase of planning has also studied Line 15’s southern extension to Fengxian New City** and the possibility of eastern extension of the Line 26 being able to run near Middle Ring Expressway.”

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p10.webp)

On March 15, 2017, Secretary General Huang Rong convened a special meeting on the extension of Line 12 and Line 15 at the municipal level. The meeting delegated the Shanghai’s department of transportation and the Municipal Bureau of Planning and Land Management to take point, with the cooperation of Shanghai Metro Co. Ltd., to complete the dedicated planning and ROW control study, (the rough Chinese equivalent of Planning and Linkages (PEL) Studies) for the extensions of Line 12 and Line 15 as soon as possible, while fully considering the relevant needs of the borough governments. In the following months, the relevant municipal and borough departments held several meetings to clarify related matters, which included the extension of Line 15 to the Wangyuan Road station of Line 5.

Between 2018 and 2019, the Municipal Planning and Resources Bureau took point in studying the planning and ROW controlling program for the southern extension of Line 15. We now have no way of knowing the different options considered for the ROW during this period, but we could gather a general sense from the public documents.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p11.webp)

The picture above is the process draft of Fengxian’s next borough level masterplan. From the image we can see that the southern extension of Line 15 travelling along Wangyuan Road, featuring five stations named (from very top red dot to one of the central dot) Fengxian Riverside, Daxie Highway, North Huancheng Road, Fengpu Avenue and Wangyuan Road, respectively.

But this version’s station spacing is obviously not very well thought out, as if the stations were arbitrarily placed from each other and the rest of the New city.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p12.webp)

In September 2018, the final released version of Fengxian’s borough level masterplan presented a much more refined station design, it is clear that the station set more in line with the highlighted planning focus of masterplan. Furthermore, Wangyuan Road station’s layout been modified to be parallel to the Metro Line 5’s existing station, with a spoon shaped bend to avoid the bridge pilers near the station.

In March 2019, the Municipal Planning and Resources Bureau publication with the number “Shanghai Planning and Resources Generald publication number [2019] No. 108”, officially announced the commencement of controlling the ROW area for the southern extension of Line 15 from unwanted development intrusions.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p13.webp)

“Starting planning southern extension of Line 15 from the existing Zizhu High-tech Park station to **Fengxian New City** station (South Hub). **The total length of the extension is about 14.7 kilometers**, travelling along South Lianhua Road - Wangyuan Road - Hangnan Highway, - Jinhai Highway. In particular, the river section of the extension contains comparative options, and this planning and ROW control study will control both ROWs of said comparative options (see text for details of the program).”

In this version, the southern extension of Line 15 starts from the existing Zizhu High-tech Park station, with Ziguang Road station, North Huancheng Road station, Fengpu Avenue station in the middle, and ending at the southern terminus of Fengxian New City station. This specific ROW contains a 5 kilometers section where lines 15 and line 5 completely overlap, but Line 15 does not stop at Jinhai Lake station, achieving a sort of “express service” on the same ROW of Line 5.

It is also said that there was a time period Line 5, Line 15 and Fengxian Line (regional rail) were running together in parallel via Jinhai Highway, but I didn't find any information to corroborate this claim.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p14.webp)

But it was also clear that building 3.3 kilometers forming a quad track outside of the city center was a complete waste of money. In subsequent studies, this one-stop express service between Wangyuan Road Station and Fengxian New city Station was quietly removed, never to be seen again in subsequent studies.

Preparation

As the southern extension of Line 15 entered full planning and ROW control in March 2019, this commencement officially marks a brand-new stage in the planning process. Fengxian borough offices and its relevant departments actively worked to move the relevant requirements of Line 15 South Extension project forward, striving for early submission to upper levels of governments for the eventual approval and commencement of construction.

At the same time, many communities regardless of being directly or indirectly served by the line in Fengxian were intrigued by the idea of Southern extension of Line 15. Let's start with the community of Xidu.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p15.webp)

This is the process draft of the Fengxian borough level masterplan we have seen above. We can see that the Fengxian Riverside Station exists at the far northern edge of the image; this location correspond roughly at the intersection of Xizha Highway and an arterial road in real life.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p16.webp)

As we can also see the station was removed in the final draft of the borough master plan. Xidu was not happy at all for the station’s removal from consideration. What happened to my perfectly good subway station?

This is because in Fengxian, the land in the borough that is north of DaYe Highway in the master plan basically all designated as undevelopable permanent farmland. Hence, according to the relevant laws, a subway station cannot be place in this farmland in principle.

Obviously, Xidu did not give up on getting a subway station in its jurisdiction area. In late November 2020, a publicly released Xidu countryside unit planning document unilaterally added a Jinzhuang Road station at the intersection Jinzhuang highway and Jinzhuang Road.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p17.webp)

Within the same timeframe, the community of Qixian's also proposed a further extension of the southern extension Line 15 eastward, to roughly where the current Jinda Highway station of Nanhui – Fengjing Regional Rail Line is located.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p18.webp)

Neither Xidu's unilaterally proposed Jinzhuang Highway station nor Qixian's unilaterally proposed extension made into the official planning application document, and at the end of 2021, the southern extension of Line 15 was submitted in accordance with the planning and ROW control design, that is cuting out the express connection to Fengxian New city station. The end result is shown in the figure below.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p19.webp)

At the end of June 2022, Shanghai Metro Phase III Modification Planning was officially approved, and the countdown to the construction commencement of the southern extension of Line 15 began.

Final preparation

On the surface, the lines submitted and approved in Shanghai Metro Phase III Modification Planning in late June 2022 all went through the same step by step process of crafting the Engineering Feasibility Study (EFS), Preliminary Design Study (PDS) stages, before obtaining final approval to start construction. It is obvious then, that it is quite improbable for the western extension of Line 12 and eastern extension of Line 13 to receive approval of their respective Engineering Feasibility studies in September 2022. It is simply impossible to prepare the thousand pages long EFS at the break-neck pace of only two months of so for these two extensions.

It turns out, many projects in Shanghai Metro Phase III Modification Planning started their EFS as early as the third quarter of 2021, long before the planning phase’s eventual approval by the NRDC.

Then, before the approval of the southern extension of Line 15 South Extension in the Shanghai Metro Phase III Modification Planning, Fengxian once again raised the need to set up provisions for an additional infill station under the permanent basic farmland of Xidu to meet the subsequent development needs of the borough. In April 2022, the borough first proposed the provision for said infill station on Jinzhuang Highway, which was rejected. Shortly thereafter, based on the “Big Zero Bay” masterplan (the masterplan for the area surrounding the 90-degree turn of the Huangpu River), the borough again proposed said provisions for the infill at the intersection of Xizha Highway and an arterial road, in which the provisions would be first utilized as a location for ventilation shafts. This time, the borough’s effort was successful.

Thus, the public was able to see a planned infill station in the released public planning document.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p20.webp)

If one have had followed the whole process of planning the southern extension of Line 15 closely, when they checked out the station location Wangyuan Road station of the southern section of the line in the published public planning document, one must have wondered: where did the spoon-like curve go?

"Spoon like curve"

The publicly released final plan showed the line 15 terminus to be directly orthogonal to the station layout of the same station of line 5.

The answer is in the planning document.

In 2021 (a rough timeframe), maybe to further adapt to the development needs of the borough, after rounds of studies Fengxian side directly modified the Nahui-Fengjing Regional Rail line from this:

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p23.webp)

(Plan for Nanhui – Fengjing Regional Rail, excerpted from Lingang FTZ’s relevant documents)

To this:

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p24.webp)

This was the final plan of the ROW. It was modified several times before reaching the stable release form. Maybe we will have a column of this line in the future.

It's been moved directly 5km north of its original 2020 ROW, so different that you can easily say that it is a completely new regional rail line.

After the Nanhui – Fengjing Regional Rail was modified into its current ROW, the regional rail’s station at Wangyuan Road occupies the land used by the south extension of Line 15. Hence, Line 15’s own Wangyuan Road station had to make way for the Nanhui – Fengjing Regional Rail station, into the L-shaped layout we see above and also removing the spoon curve leading up to the station.

As for the original intention of the spoon curve to bypass the bridge piles. One bridge bypass remained successful, while two other bridges needed dismantling and reconstruction after line 15’s southern extension is complete.

Finally, the ROW of southern extension of Line 15 is finalized and stable, and until the start of construction, there would be no further major changes.

In December 24, 2023, construction for the southern extension of Line 15 has commenced.

![【英文翻译】15号线南延伸规划变迁](/images/deleted/line15-south-ext-evolution-en/p26.webp)

Outlook.

Since the southern extension of Line 15 was officially proposed in 2017, it took just six years to successfully finalize the proposal and launching construction in 2023, a rarity even in the Shanghai’s history of subway construction and can be considered quite efficient.

The needs of the communities along the line were also satisfactorily met: Xidu got provisions for an infill station, while Qixian got a stop on the Nanhui-Fengjing Regional Rail, a merry ending to the story.

The only notable regret might be that, due to various reasons (e.g. the borough spent a extensive long time on adding the provision for the Xizha Highway infill station and the necessary engineering provisions for a future southern extension, ultimately successful), the southern extension of Line 15, which was originally planned to start at the end of 2023 along with the western extension of Line 12 and eastern extension of Line 13, was delayed by 1 year, before construction could began. Revenue service for these 3 extensions are expected to be start in 2028, though there is now news that the southern extension of Line 15 South Extension could be open for revenue service half a year to one year ahead of schedule.

The story of Line 15 South extension does not end here. Due to the design changes at Wangyuan Road station from being parallel to the Line 5 station to perpendicular of the Line 5 station and Nanhui-Fengjing Regional Rail station, coupled with Fengxian’s successful political maneuvering, the southern extension of Line 15 now has the necessary engineering provisions for another southern extension along Wangyuan Road. Haiwan University area, Old Fengxian, Fengxian South transport hub have all been put as the ideal destination for the south southern extension line 15.
